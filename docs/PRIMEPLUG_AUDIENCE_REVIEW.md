# Primeplug audience review — preliminary, not a legal determination

## Scope and limitations

The owner was unsure whether the elephant and Chiitan Shorts were intended to target children under 13. This is a **preliminary review**, not a recorded legal determination or authorization to change any YouTube setting. Evidence reviewed: the original saved video titles, narration scripts, hashtags and descriptions in the `automation-state` queue; saved rendered thumbnails for Chiitan and the elephant; sample frames from their public source-footage windows; and the previous frame-based editorial audit of the two NASA clips. YouTube blocked anonymous playback during the later check, so the complete current public videos, current live descriptions and present audience settings have not been independently verified. Source frames do not prove what survived the final crop. A saved upload package does not prove that a live description was not subsequently edited. No existing video, metadata, visibility, or audience setting was changed during this review.

YouTube's guidance: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en . A family-friendly video that might be watched by children is not automatically made for kids. Content that targets children as one of its audiences may be made for kids even where they are not the primary audience; the complete facts, including subject, language, characters, marketing and intended audience, matter. If still unsure, consider qualified legal advice. Children's ages and applicable law can vary by location.

## Safety change prepared locally

The revised guarded uploader requires an explicit audience choice tied to the same approved candidate ID and exact SHA-256; unknown or missing choices hold the upload. The private smoke uploader also requires that exact per-video approval and a reviewed audience. The low-level YouTube uploader refuses a missing/non-boolean audience. `verify-published.mjs` records both API-returned `selfDeclaredMadeForKids` and `madeForKids`, or null when absent, and never changes settings. No channel, public-publishing setting, or production/rights gate is changed by the review.

The workflow choices are `review_required` (default, holds upload), `made_for_kids`, or `not_made_for_kids`. A choice is an explicit human decision, not proof that it is legally correct. These changes must be committed and pushed to `editorial-evidence-hold` before the live workflow uses them; local tests alone do not deploy anything.

Local evidence: focused audience and publisher tests passed; `PATH=/home/user/bin:$PATH npm test` passed **388/388** including mocked upload and override checks, with no real YouTube upload. The first unmodified-PATH full-suite attempt had five FFmpeg-not-found failures and 17 skips; the successful rerun used the existing FFmpeg and FFprobe in `/home/user/bin`. Node in this workspace was v20.20.2; GitHub's configured runtime is Node 22, and no GitHub run is claimed by this local result.

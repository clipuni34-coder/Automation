## How to use the suggestions

- **First two seconds:** Treat 75% stayed as an aspirational test, not a universal algorithmic requirement. If the stayed rate is weak and there is enough data, test a more immediate, truthful visual opening.
- **Duration and replays:** Try 15–20 seconds when the full story naturally fits. A percentage viewed above 100% can occur when viewers rewatch; it is not proof of a seamless loop or a promise of reach. Compare the actual ending and beginning, without trimming away the payoff or final narration just to loop.
- **Under 80% viewed:** Inspect where people leave and test tighter editing; do not automatically cut a video or infer a cause from a single percentage.
- **Engagement:** A simple relevant choice question may be drafted for review. No comment is posted or pinned automatically, and made-for-kids videos may have comments unavailable.
- **Cadence:** YouTube's suggested 3–5 uploads a week is a planning target. The existing daily production schedule is unchanged, and each upload still requires explicit approval tied to its candidate, exact video hash and reviewed audience.

The metrics are observational and may be delayed, revised, unavailable or based on a small audience. Strong retention does not guarantee viral reach.

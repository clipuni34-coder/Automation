# Planning evidence credits

The contact sheets in this folder are sampled still frames from the following videos. The frames were resized and arranged into labelled sheets for editorial review; they do not represent continuous viewing. These credits do not replace the separate credit and licence notice required with any resulting video.

- `planning-cat-nesnad-*`: Nesnad, [Tonkinese cat rolling around – Japan – 2025 mar 22](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tonkinese_cat_rolling_around_-_Japan_-_2025_mar_22.webm), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: sampled, resized, time-labelled and arranged as contact sheets.
- `planning-cat-taiwan-*`: MiNe, [Cat playing in Taiwan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cat_playing_in_Taiwan.webm), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Changes: sampled, resized, time-labelled and arranged as contact sheets.
